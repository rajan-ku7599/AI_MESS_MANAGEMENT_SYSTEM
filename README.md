
# 🍽️ AI Mess Management System

## 📌 Project Overview

The **AI Mess Management System** is a web-based platform designed to simplify and digitize the daily operations of a college hostel mess or institutional dining facility. The system provides a centralized solution for managing meal bookings, attendance, daily menus, payments, complaints, and user records through a user-friendly interface.

The website includes three separate dashboards for **Students, Staff, and Admin**, each with role-based access and specific functionalities. Students can view menus, book meals, track attendance, check payment details, and submit complaints. Staff members can manage meal-related operations and monitor student attendance, while administrators can manage users, approve registrations, update menus, monitor payments, and oversee the overall mess activities.

The project aims to reduce paperwork, minimize manual errors, improve transparency, and make mess management more efficient. It also provides a foundation for integrating AI-powered features such as meal demand prediction, smart meal planning, automated complaint prioritization, and data-driven reporting.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Role-Based Authentication:** Separate login and dashboard access for Students, Staff, and Admin.
- **Student Dashboard:** View personal information, meal bookings, attendance records, payment details, and complaints.
- **Meal Booking Management:** Book meals according to the available schedule and manage daily meal requirements.
- **Attendance Management:** Maintain and monitor student meal attendance digitally.
- **Dynamic Menu Management:** View daily menus and allow authorized users to add, update, or remove menu items.
- **Payment Management:** Track meal charges, payment records, and outstanding balances.
- **Complaint Management:** Submit complaints, monitor their status, and help staff and administrators manage issues efficiently.
- **User Management:** Manage student and staff accounts with registration approval and rejection workflows.
- **Admin Dashboard:** Monitor mess activities, user records, meal bookings, attendance, payments, and complaints from a centralized dashboard.
- **Staff Dashboard:** Provide staff with tools to manage assigned mess operations and student meal records.
- **Real-Time Data Management:** Support centralized data storage and synchronization using Firebase services.
- **AI Integration Potential:** Support future enhancements such as meal demand forecasting, smart food planning, and intelligent complaint classification.
- **Responsive User Interface:** Provide a clean and accessible interface for everyday mess management.

## 👥 User Roles

### 🎓 1. Student
- Register and access the student dashboard.
- View daily breakfast, lunch, and dinner menus.
- Book available meals.
- Check meal attendance and booking history.
- View payment information.
- Submit complaints and track their status.

### 👨‍🍳 2. Staff
- Access the staff dashboard.
- View relevant student and meal records.
- Manage meal attendance and assigned operations.
- Review and respond to complaints according to permissions.
- Assist in maintaining accurate mess records.

### 🛡️ 3. Admin
- Access the central administration dashboard.
- Approve or reject student and staff registrations.
- Manage user accounts and roles.
- Add, update, and delete meal menu items.
- Monitor bookings, attendance, and payment records.
- Review complaints and track their resolution.
- Oversee the overall functioning of the mess management system.

## 🤖 AI-Powered Enhancement Ideas

The system can be extended with intelligent features to improve operational efficiency:

- **Intelligent Menu Suggestions:** Suggest menu combinations based on meal history and available data.
